# The World of Cehiri

## The Long Night

174 years ago, the boundary between worlds shattered. This catastrophic event, known as [[The Long Night]], opened Cehiri to the greater Beyond. The realm barrier was torn asunder and rifts emerged alongside mana bringing forth Mankind's greatest tribulation yet.

Scholars still debate the cause. Some claim a failed ritual by a cabal of archmages attempting to tap into a new source of power. Others believe it was intentional, an act by forces beyond the veil. A few fringe theorists suggest Cehiri's world spirit itself tore the boundary open.

Whatever the cause, the results were catastrophic and transformative.

The first few decades were called the [[Age of Ruin]]. Portals ripped open across the world. Creatures poured through. Magic density spiked so dramatically that ley lines could be seen with the naked eye. The old nations crumbled. Billions died.

But the mortal rages emerged from the ashes, anger and retribution held with their eyes as they roared against the sky and raised their weapons.

Now, nearly 175 years later, the world exists in tense equilibrium. New nations have risen from the dust, ancient mysteries of the past have stepped forth from the shadows. It is an era of change, of warfare, of light against the darkness.

The invaders have been pushed back to contested zones but never truly defeated. And every year, the boundary grows thinner, allowing stronger entities to cross over. It's a battle of attrition, and one we are slowly losing.

This is where you step in O brave students of Zenith, shine like the brightest of stars and cast your light upon the Myriad Races and show that mankind will not go quietly into the night. 

## Gifts

The events of [[The Long Night]] awoke special metaphysical abilities laying dormant within each soul. These abilities, colloquially called Gifts, manifest typically early on in life and have become a part of daily life in Cehiri. 

Before the Sundering, Gifts were rare and limited in usage such as bending a spoon with one's mind. Now they are universal, awakened by the same catastrophe that tore the boundary apart.

Some Gifts are [[Lineage Gifts]], passed down through family lines with variations. These often predate the Sundering by centuries, though the Sundering strengthened them.

Your Gift is a part of who you are, some even think its a representation of a facet of one's soul. At Zenith Academy, students learn to develop their Gifts alongside traditional magic and combat techniques, preparing them for the battle that lays ahead.

*Note: Clearing dungeons can sometimes increase the strength of a Gift through absorbed [[Essence (Gift)]].*

## [[Technology]]

The technology level of that in Cehiri is that of modern fantasy, at or above our current level of technology. Here both magic and technology are blended into a modern fantasy society. Some common things you might see are:

- **Scrolls:** Magical communication devices used for tracking currency, sending messages, browsing the aethernet, and more (essentially magic phones)
- **Swifts:** Flying vehicles for transportation (similar to air cars)
- **Leviathans:** Large flying vessels for long-distance travel
- **The Aethernet:** Magical information network (like the internet)

Cities are protected by magical [[Hecatean Wards]] that detect and repel Incursor incursions.

There is a lot more for you to discover within the world as well.

## [[Guilds]]

Professional organizations that handle various threats and services. All manner of guilds exist, ranging from extremely public guilds run like a company, to private, or even family owned guilds. 

Guilds usually specialize in one or more areas such as forging weapons, fighting against [[Incursors]], construction, exploring dungeons, and so on and so forth. The sky is truly the limit. 

Guild membership is a common career path for Academy graduates.

## [[Dungeons]]

Spatial anomalies that appeared after the Sundering. Dungeons:
- Fold space and create unique environments
- Connect to fragments of myriad realms
- Contain creatures and resources
- Are graded by danger level (F through S)
- Clearing them occasionally grants Essence that can strengthen Gifts

## [[Lost Zones]]

Territories too dangerous for normal habitation, classified by danger level:
- **Grey Zones:** Dangerous but navigable with preparation
- **Red Zones:** Extremely dangerous, professional teams only
- **Black Zones:** Effectively no-go areas

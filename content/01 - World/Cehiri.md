# The World of Cehiri

## The Long Night

174 years ago, the boundary between worlds shattered. This catastrophic event, known as [[The Long Night]], opened Cehiri to the greater Beyond. The realm barrier was torn asunder and rifts emerged alongside mana bringing forth Mankind's greatest tribulation yet.

The Sundering:
- Opened rifts between worlds
- Awakened supernatural "Gifts" in all people
- Introduced existential threats to the world
- Triggered the Age of Ruin that followed

## Gifts

Every person in Cehiri possesses a [[Gift]], a unique supernatural ability awakened by the Sundering. Gifts vary wildly in power and application.

Some Gifts are [[Lineage Gifts]], passed down through family lines with variations. These often predate the Sundering by centuries, though the Sundering strengthened them.

Clearing dungeons can sometimes increase the strength of a Gift through absorbed [[Essence (Gift)]].

## [[Technology]]

Cehiri blends magic and technology into a modern fantasy society:

- **Scrolls:** Magical communication devices (essentially magic phones)
- **Swifts:** Flying vehicles for transportation
- **Leviathans:** Large flying vessels for long-distance travel
- **The Aethernet:** Magical information network

## [[Guilds]]

Professional organizations that handle various threats and services. Guild membership is a common career path for Academy graduates.

## [[Dungeons]]

Spatial anomalies that appeared after the Sundering. Dungeons:
- Fold space and create unique environments
- Connect to fragments of myriad realms
- Contain creatures and resources
- Are graded by danger level (F through S)
- Clearing them occasionally grants Essence that can strengthen Gifts

## [[Lost Zones]]

Territories too dangerous for normal habitation, classified by danger level:
- **Grey Zones:** Dangerous but navigable with preparation
- **Red Zones:** Extremely dangerous, professional teams only
- **Black Zones:** Effectively no-go areas

## The Threats

Demons from the REDACTED, the primary antagonist faction. They emerged through rifts opened by the Sundering and seek to consume or corrupt the mortal world.

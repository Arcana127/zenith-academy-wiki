## The World of Cehiri

  

250 years ago, a catastrophic event called the Sundering tore holes between worlds, reshaping continents, collapsing nations, and flooding reality with magical energy. The old world had seven continents. The new world has five, plus countless island chains and the Shattered Reaches where reality itself is unstable.

  

The Sundering awakened latent supernatural potential in virtually all mortals. These abilities, called Gifts, manifest typically during puberty, though late awakenings into one's twenties happen. Gifts are unique to the individual and vary enormously in power. Some let someone heat their coffee. Others level city blocks. Some Gifts run in bloodlines, called Lineage Gifts.

  

Gift categories include Elemental (fire, water, earth, lightning, etc.), Physical (strength, speed, transformation), Mental (telepathy, telekinesis, cognition), Spatial (teleportation, pocket dimensions), and Esoteric (luck, momentum, probability, decay, growth).

  

The current year is 250 AE (Aurelian Era).

## The Aurelian Calendar

  

Adopted after the Sundering as a unified timekeeping system. 12 months of 30 days each, plus 5 Hollow Days between years. One sun, two moons.

  

**Moons:**

- Aelora (the Greater Moon): Silver-white, 30-day cycle, reliable

- Vexis (the Lesser Moon): Pale amber, 47-day cycle, irregular. When both are full simultaneously it's called a Conjunction, roughly twice per year, considered either lucky or ominous

  

**Days of the Week (7 days):** Firstday, Dustday, Windway, Hearthday, Tournday, Marketday, Stillday

  

**Months:** (Details from our calendar conversation, 12 months of 30 days spanning three academic terms plus breaks. Campaign begins late Goldfall, 250 AE.)

## Continents and Nations

  

### Aurenis (analogous to Europe and western Asia)

The most densely populated continent. Hit hard during the Age of Ruin but recovered faster than most due to its concentration of pre-Sundering magical institutions.

  

Major nations:

- **The Valtorian Compact** - A federation of city-states united under a council of Guildmasters. The single largest political entity on Aurenis and a major sponsor of Zenith Academy.

- **Kharend** - A militaristic nation that has made reclaiming Lost Zones its national identity. Kharendian soldiers are renowned for discipline and integration of tech with martial arts. Origin of Silas Varen.

- **The Sovereignty of Illmara** - A magocracy ruled by a council of archmages. They claim to be inheritors of the pre-Sundering magical tradition and guard their secrets jealously. Origin of Lucien Thorne.

  

### Novareth (analogous to the Americas)

A vast continent split into northern and southern landmasses connected by a narrow isthmus. More untamed wilderness than any other continent and some of the largest Lost Zones. Home to some of the most powerful dungeons discovered to date.

  

Major nations:

- **The Ashford Republic** - A young nation built on meritocracy and innovation. Leads the world in magitechnology development.

- **The Crest Dominion** - Ruled by powerful families whose bloodline Gifts have been cultivated for generations. Aristocratic and traditional, they view their Gifts as divine mandate.

  

### Zha'Keth (analogous to Africa and the Middle East)

A continent of extremes: scorching deserts, lush jungles, towering plateaus. First continent to successfully establish lasting truces with certain Incursor factions, most notably the Auroch Clans. This has made them pariahs in some circles but given them unique advantages.

  

### Meridia (analogous to East and Southeast Asia)

An archipelago continent of thousands of islands. Unmatched naval power with unique maritime magic. The boundary between worlds is particularly thin here, making it both blessed with magical resources and cursed with frequent portal breaches. Origin of Mirabelle Solenne.

  

### Polheim (analogous to Australia/Oceania plus Antarctica)

The southernmost continent, largely frozen and sparsely populated. Home to ancient ruins predating the earliest mortal civilizations, suggesting the Sundering may not have been the first time the boundaries weakened. Expeditions into the interior rarely return, but those that do bring artifacts of immense power. Origin of Veyra Ashwood.

  

### The Lost Zones

Approximately 30% of Cehiri's landmass is classified as Lost Zones where mortal civilization has collapsed.

  

Threat levels:

- **Grey Zones** - Dangerous but navigable. Guild teams regularly operate here.

- **Red Zones** - Extremely dangerous. Only elite teams enter, usually for specific objectives.

- **Black Zones** - Presumed uninhabitable. Reality itself may be compromised. The Nethrim control several.

## The Incursors

  

All entities that have crossed from other realms are collectively called Incursors. They are not unified.

  

### The Nethrim (Primary Threat)

The most powerful and organized Incursors, from the Obsidian Threshold, a realm of black stone and burning skies. They appear vaguely humanoid but wrong. Their forms shift at the edges.

  

Hierarchy:

- **Lesser Nethrim** - Foot soldiers, individually manageable

- **Greater Nethrim** - Tactical threats requiring trained teams

- **Dusk Lords** - Commanders of immense power, each ruling territory

- **Sovereigns** - Reality-warping entities whose mere presence is cataclysmic

  

### Secondary Factions

- **The Hollow Swarm** - Insectoid entities from a hive-realm. No negotiation, only consumption. Terrifyingly numerous.

- **The Auroch Clans** - Massive bestial warriors who respect strength. Occasionally parley with mortals. Some mercenary Auroch have been hired by desperate factions.

- **The Whispering Court** - Ethereal beings focused on manipulation rather than conquest. Offer bargains, grant boons, collect debts. Their relationship with the Nethrim is unclear, possibly adversarial. Aldric Brennan's sleeper implant comes from them.

- **Feral Incursors** - Unaffiliated monsters from other realms that crossed over and now roam wild zones.



## Technology

  

- **Scrolls** - Magical smartphones. Flexible, palm-sized enchanted sheets for messaging, Aethernet access, maps, and utility functions. Academy issues them to students with limited features.

- **The Aethernet** - Magical information network connecting major cities. Free basic access for news, forums, research, communication, entertainment. Premium content, specialized databases, and restricted archives require subscriptions or authorization.

- **Aircars and Leviathans** - Personal and mass air transport. Leviathans are large aircraft.

- **Synthesis Forges** - Fabricate objects from raw materials using magic and technology. Limited by complexity and operator skill.

- **Medical Pods** - Heal injuries in hours. Cannot regrow limbs or resurrect the dead without expensive specialized intervention.

- **Hecatean Wards** - Protective magical barriers named for Hecate. Protect buildings, cities, and vehicles. Ward strength indicates wealth and importance.

- **Anchor Stations** - Massive installations stabilizing the boundary between worlds, reducing portal breach frequency. Expensive, protecting only the most valuable locations.

  

### Dungeons

Spatial anomalies connecting to fragments of other realms. Manifest spontaneously, contain monsters and treasures, and have a Core that must be claimed or destroyed. The World Spirit of Cehiri rewards clearers with Essence, pure magical energy that strengthens Gifts. Graded F through S, plus theoretical Mythic grade (never successfully cleared).

## Primus

  

The floating city built on the shell of REIMEI, an ancient Atlas Turtle. Population roughly 350,000. Shell spans approximately 20km diameter with about 35km of livable city space and 5km for infrastructure.

  

Verenthax follows a semi-predictable migration path, occasionally stopping near coastlines for resupply and trade. These coastal stops are when larger field trips happen. Teleportation to and from Primus is possible but costly due to the turtle's frequent movement and the Hecatean Wards.

  

### Districts (from lower tiers upward)

- **Titan's Gate** - Harbor, entry point, port authority, customs, arriving travelers

- **Brackworth** - Working-class district, industry, forges, practical shops, laborers' housing

- **The Warrens** - Unofficial district in the underside/gaps. Black markets, underground services, forgotten people. Crime is organized, subtle, and rare.

- **The Vermillion Quarters** - Commercial and entertainment hub. Restaurants, theaters, shopping, nightlife.

- **Auspice Row** - Student district between the academy and the Vermillion Quarters. Guild recruitment, student housing, social scene.

- **Ablaster Terrace** - Wealthy residential area. Manors, elite residences, old money.

- **The Sanctum** - Zenith Academy campus at the apex.

  

### Infrastructure

The Anchors (ward maintenance), The Terraces (agriculture), The Basins (water collection and purification, also draws from sea), Shell Watch, The Ramparts, Old Foundation, The Scarfields.

  

### Transportation

Color-coded tram lines including the Silver Line through Auspice Row. Lifts connecting tiers. Aircars for wealthier residents.

  

### Security

The Sentinels (law enforcement), Ward Guardians, and the Hecatean Ward network. Crime on Primus is extremely rare and organized, usually dealing with black market goods and underground services.

  

### Currency

The Common Mark (standardized across Cehiri). Coins in copper (1), silver (10), gold (100), platinum (1,000). Can be attached to scrolls for digital transactions with personal identification security.

## Auspice Row (Heavy Detail)

  

A two-kilometer stretch between the academy gates and the Vermillion Quarters. Named for an old shrine where students sought good omens before exams. Architecture is a charming mix of old stone and modern glass connected by covered walkways.

  

### Key Landmarks

- **The Promenade** - Main thoroughfare with the Silver Line tram

- **The Steps** - Where Auspice Row meets the Sanctum, a natural gathering spot with sunset views

- **Threshold Plaza** - Southern end, fountain depicting Zenrathi conversing with Verenthax, seasonal markets

- **The Shrine of First Steps** - The original shrine, students still touch it for luck

- **Gordo's Corner** - Intersection named for the legendary noodle shop

- **Inkwell Lane** - Narrow street of bookshops and academic suppliers

- **Crucible Court** - Square where combat equipment shops cluster

- **Memorial Arch** - Inscribed with names of students killed in an Incursor attack 32 years ago

- **The Overhang** - Section where facing buildings nearly meet overhead, perpetual twilight

  

### Notable Shops and NPCs

  

**Combat and Equipment:**

- **Vance and Hollow Outfitters** - Three-story equipment shop. Run by Seren Vance (no relation to infirmary Vances). Reads fighting styles at a glance. Standard gear ground floor, advanced second, custom third by appointment.

- **Korrin's Edge** - Bladesmith Korrin Ashfall forges unenchanted blades. Refuses to sell to first-years. Two-month waiting list.

- **The Bent Shield** - Secondhand equipment. Run by Tomas Reyes, who knows the story behind every item.

  

**Academic and Magical:**

- **Resonance and Rune** - Magic supplies. Daven Kohl offers free tutoring despite being an awkward genius.

- **The Steady Hand** - Tattoo artist Zara Ink does magical tattoos with high standards.

- **The Annotated Page** - Bookseller Mira Solvei with uncanny recommendations.

- **The Dusty Tome** - Used books. Hazel Briggs claims to have a copy of everything written about Verenthax.

- **Quill and Query** - Stationer Old Deren, legendary handwriting guides.

  

**Food and Drink:**

- **Gordo's** - Legendary noodle shop. Gordo Chen has enhanced taste. Best value in the district. Refuses to expand, raise prices, or explain recipes. Seats thirty, lines during peak.

- **The Gilded Ladle** - Upscale. Chef Aurelius Venn offers scholar's discounts for culinary trivia.

- **The Brass Kettle** - Tea house. Mei-Lin Park senses emotional states and recommends teas accordingly. Upstairs loft is silence-only study space.

- **The Rusty Anchor** - Tavern. Brennan Cole is a former Zenith dropout who doesn't discuss why he left. Trivia nights, performances, cohort challenges.

- **The Morning After** - Breakfast spot. Delia Marsh offers free "Survivor's Specials."

  

**Specialty:**

- **The Wandering Star** - Curiosity shop. Ezra Nighthollow's inventory changes mysteriously and includes items that shouldn't exist.

- **Familiar Grounds** - Pet shop. Willem Thorn (not related to the professor) requires proof of competence before selling.

- **The Woven Thread** - Clothier Lira Vance, uniform alterations and custom wear.

- **The Broken Clock** - Repairs anything. Magnus Stern can fix but not create.

- **Dreams for Sale** - Sleep-related enchanted objects. Somnia refuses to sell anything that lets someone enter another's dreams without consent.

- **The Quick Copy** - Print and reproduction. Twins Ash and Ember Croft, including discretion services.

- **The Verdant Mortar** - Apothecary. Yua Tanaka sells exam aids discreetly.

## Zenith Academy

  

Founded roughly 146 years ago by Archmage Niavor Zenrathi during the Age of Ruin. Originally a sanctuary, now one of the world's premier hero academies. Four-year program with a cohort system (groups of 4-6). Track specializations: Combat, Research, Support, Reconnaissance, Command. The Star system designates elite graduates.

  

Academic currency called Marks (distinct from the Common Mark used in Cehiri). Trimester system spanning 10 months with breaks. Campaign starts during Arrival Week in late Goldfall, 250 AE.

  

### Campus Facilities

  

**The Spire of Zenrathi** - 20-level administrative tower, tallest structure on Verenthax.

- Levels 1-2: Ceremonial entrance, Great Hall

- Levels 3-5: Administrative offices

- Levels 6-10: Faculty offices and meeting spaces

- Levels 11-15: Archives (restricted access)

- Levels 16-19: Guest quarters for dignitaries and visiting lecturers

- Level 20: Headmaster's Office with the Vigil Window (270-degree enchanted glass view), Consultation Circle, and private quarters

- Observation Deck: Open to students during daylight, great views

  

**The Collegium** - Interconnected academic buildings.

- The Centrum: Oldest building, most first-year instruction. Features The Gathering (atrium), Lecture Hall Primus (seats 400), smaller halls A through F.

- Thorne Hall: Named for a benefactor, houses research and seminar rooms.

- Ashford Building: Modern construction, specialized lab spaces.

- Memorial Wing: Honors fallen alumni.

- Synthesis Wing: Enchanting, alchemy, runecraft, magitechnology labs.

  

**The Crucible Complex** - Practical training.

- Grand Crucible: Arena seating 20,000 with configurable terrain for major tournaments.

- Five Combat Halls for daily training.

- Outdoor training fields.

  

**The Proving Grounds** - Multi-level underground training dungeon. A stabilized C-grade dungeon that regenerates weekly with adjustable difficulty. Monitored by Nyx, an owl entity from the realm of Velathos.

  

**The Athenaeum** - The library, one of the largest magical knowledge collections in the world. Four stacks including a Restricted Collection. Managed by Edith Holloway, whose Gift is Intent Reading.

  

**The Infirmary** - Four-tier medical pod system. Run by Theodora Vance with her adopted granddaughter Lily Vance.

  

### Residential Areas

- **Foundation Halls** - First-years, five buildings

- **The Crossroads** - Second-years, organized by Track

- **The Heights** - Third-years with mission prep facilities

- **The Threshold** - Fourth-years, most privacy and independence

- **Ascension Hall** - Star program housing with superior amenities and private training grounds

  

### Dining and Social

- **The Great Hall** - Seats 3,000, secondary options and after-hours basics available

- **The Sanctuary** - Multi-faith meditation and worship space

- **The Grove, Founder's Garden, Verenthax Overlook** - Outdoor spaces

  

### Transportation

The Circuit tram system, lift towers connecting to Primus districts.

  

### Student Clubs (Sampling)

- The Proving Ground Delvers (fastest dungeon clear times)

- Artificer's Circle (enchanting and magitechnology showcase)

- The Zenith Chronicle (student newspaper)

- Dueling Club (competitive combat)

- Monster Research Society (field research with supervision)

- The Warrens Outreach Program (volunteer tutoring, quietly controversial)

- Starseeker Society (unofficial Star program networking)

## First-Year Professors

  

### Silas Varen (Combat Fundamentals)

Age 35, looks 55-60. Grizzled veteran with a steel prosthetic arm. Hard but fair. Gift: Soul Furnace (converts physical damage into temporary power). From Kharend.

  

### Mirabelle Solenne (Gift Development / Homeroom)

Age 32. Red hair, blue eyes. Warm but competitive. Gift: Equilibrium Sight combined with Harmonic Flame. From Meridia. Has history with Silas. Observes Covenant traditions casually.

  

### Lucien Thorne (Magical Theory)

Age 23. Young genius who'd rather be researching. Has a TA named Arcanis. Gift removes his need for sleep. From the Sovereignty of Illmara.

  

### Aldric Brennan (Incursor Studies and History)

Age 173, looks early 40s. Kind immortal who actually lived through the Sundering. Secretly a sleeper agent with an implant from the Whispering Court (unknown even to him). Claims citizenship in the Valtorian Compact though he predates it. Has complicated views on divinity.

  

### Veyra Ashwood (Dungeon Ecology)

Age 29. Cold exterior, void-black eyes, silver-blonde hair. Gift: Abyssal Collapse (cursed, tied to spatial distortion). Hidden soft side. From Polheim. Maintains a Hecatean shrine in her quarters.

## Player Characters

  

### Solari/Lunari Caelum (Player: Kola)

Age 19 (youngest). Sorcerer. Formerly Seren Caelum. Two personalities sharing one body after a ritual gone wrong five years ago. Solari channels light, sun, and positive emotions. Lunari channels darkness, moon, and negative emotions. A pendant stabilizes switching; if overused it cracks, risking an "eclipse state." The original personality Seren is dormant deep within, chained between the two. Solari wants to be whole again. Lunari is spiteful and resistant.

  

Their parents were manipulated into approving the ritual. The manipulator's identity is an open plot hook.

  

### Elodie Caelum (Player: Alex)

Age 21. Fighter with Gunslinger Dedication. Solari/Lunari's older sister. 4'9", coquette/academia style. Gift: Speed/Durability Swap (enhanced speed, jump, fall damage reduction, or consume speed for tankiness). Wields a pen weapon that transforms into bat/gun, covered in stickers. Bubbly, loud, protective, has a temper. Childhood best friends with Pyrus. Holds the ritual against her parents.

  

### Pyrus Moore (Player: Brandon)

Age 21. Thaumaturge. Cursed by a Lovecraftian entity acting as a barrier between the void and the boundary. The entity's secret name "Moryn" has minor effects but no summoning power. His Gift, Eldritch Perception, was slightly altered by the entity. Wields his father's fire axe. Grey cat familiar with green eyes. Family is prominent locally (father is fire chief). Deal with the entity is the family's deepest secret. Slowly losing humanity but gaining understanding. Childhood best friends with Elodie.

  

### Evelynn "Eve" Marie Hertz (Player: Katelin)

Age 20. Gunslinger wielding a large cannon. Built by Heinrich for revenge after Nethrim killed his wife and daughter. An android "wished alive through a Gift," making her nearly one of a kind. Discovered her purpose, ran off to fulfill it, was severely damaged. Appearance is passably human but noticeably android up close. Trauma centers on failure. Trust with Heinrich is healing but fragile. Believes she exists to destroy; needs to discover she's meant to protect. Connected to Zenith through Theodora Vance.

  

### Party Connections

- The Caelum family had a vacation home near where the Moores lived. Pyrus and Elodie met as kids and became best friends, solidified when Pyrus's father extinguished a fire at the Caelum home.

- Their older siblings are also connected: Miranda Moore (4th year, light/order Gift, told a partial truth about Pyrus) and Alice Caelum (4th year, friends with Miranda).

- Eve enters as the outsider with no prior connection to the others. Her arc involves earning her place.

## Opening Sequence

  

The campaign begins during Arrival Week in late Goldfall, 250 AE. A planned orientation simulation malfunctions (by design, to test how students handle the unexpected), giving the four-person cohort a chance to bond while keeping the academy appearing competent rather than negligent.
